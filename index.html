Case study · 01

Ad teams spend $50M/year on creative and still can't keep up with the volume their media buyers need.

Creative IQ · Agentic AI · AdTech
The story

Media buyers can run 40 ad variations a day. Creative teams produce 4. The gap isn't staffing. It's architecture.

Creative IQ is the orchestration layer. Brief goes in, performance-tuned variations come out. Every asset is tagged against the brand book and the KPI it's optimized for. Specialized agents handle concept generation, script writing, video synthesis, brand-compliance audit, and performance scoring.

Brief + brand book→Concept agent
↓ N variations, parallelized
Script→Shot plan→Video synthesis (Sora/Runway/Kling)
Rendered assets→Brand-compliance ML→PASS / REWRITE
Approved→Perf scoring→Feedback into next brief
What's next
  • Per-brand fine-tuning. LoRA adapters per client so brand rules are learned, not prompted.
  • Platform-native rendering. Dedicated post-processing for Reels, TikTok, Shorts.
  • Cost-aware model routing. Pick Sora vs Kling based on budget + creative importance.

Case study · 02

Clinic staff spend 45 minutes Ctrl+F'ing through PDFs to answer a single prior auth question.

AuthLookup · GenAI · Healthcare
The story

Prior authorization is a $35B/year administrative burden. The answers live in 90MB of CMS policy PDFs. The people who need them are on a phone with a patient.

AuthLookup is the plain-English interface to CMS. 4,179 CPT codes parsed with real Medicare coverage requirements. Body-part-aware matching. Contrast-protocol logic. FHIR R4 compliant responses ready for EHR integration. Runs 100% locally on Ollama. Zero API costs. PHI never leaves the machine.

User query→Ollama (local)→Intent + CPT candidates
CPT candidates→ChromaDB→Answer + FHIR R4

Case study · 03

A third of the food we grow rots before anyone eats it. Most of it rots because logistics runs on phone calls.

FreshFlow · Agentic AI · CPG
The story

Food distribution is both enormous and analog. Surplus strawberries? Call retailers until one says yes. Truck breaks down? Re-route by hand.

FreshFlow is a multi-agent platform that runs those coordination loops. Three specialist agents: a forecaster, a spoilage risk scorer, and a reroute negotiator. They share state, not chat. Humans approve proposals before execution. Built with a team of five in 72 hours for the Amazon Nova Hackathon.

Supplier feeds→Forecast agent→Demand by SKU
Inventory→Spoilage agent→Risk scores
Shared state→Reroute agent→Proposals→Human approval
What's next
  • Real supplier integrations. SAP, NetSuite, distributor TMS systems.
  • Learned negotiator. Replace rules+LLM with a model that learns cost/risk/relationship trade-offs.
  • Agent trace viewer. Purpose-built observability for multi-agent coordination.

Case study · 04

Every PM scrolls through 200 tickets, 40 Slack threads, and 12 transcripts trying to remember what users want.

Cursor for PMs · GenAI · Product Intelligence
The story

The feedback exists. The patterns exist. The problem is the human brain can't hold 300 tickets in working memory while writing a PRD.

Cursor for PMs is the memory layer. Ingest every feedback channel into a persistent context graph. Ask "what are users saying about feature X?" and get cited evidence. V2 rebuilt with LangGraph: the LLM plans whether to do lookup, aggregation, clustering, or temporal comparison based on the question type.

Tickets + Slack + reviews + calls→Ingestion→Feedback store
↓ pgvector + full-text + topic clusters
PM question→Query planner (LangGraph)
Plan→Retrieval→Aggregation→Cited synthesis
What's next
  • Insight to spec to code. Generate PRD drafts, then agent-executable specs.
  • Per-customer embeddings. Fine-tuned models for product-specific jargon.
  • Proactive alerts. "Onboarding complaints spiked 3x this week."
